Our Approach

Phase 01
01

Optimise the theme and scripts

Make the product image and key content load first, and clear out anything in the theme that slows the page down.

→
Phase 02
02

Gate non-tracking apps, then maintain the score

Non-tracking apps load at the right moment instead of all at once. Tracking apps are not touched.

How we'll get there

01

Theme and script optimisation

We fine-tune how the product page loads so shoppers see the product sooner. No change to design or content.

02

Agreed app list

Before we start, your team shares the list of apps we may work on. Tracking apps are not touched at all.

03

Gating non-tracking apps

Apps like reviews, chat and rewards load once the shopper starts to scroll or tap. They work exactly as before.

04

Go-live and checks

Cart, Buy Now, checkout, login and tracking are tested before and after go-live, then taken live on the default product template.
